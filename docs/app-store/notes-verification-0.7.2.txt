VERSION 0.7.2: UPDATES OF THE EXERCISE ENGINE
Exercises come from MathALÉA, an open-source maths exercise generator by CoopMaths, an association of maths teachers funded by the French Ministry of Education (DNE): https://forge.apps.education.fr/coopmaths/mathalea . It changes every day: 811 merge requests merged in the last 30 days, each reviewed by a teacher, mostly new exercises or fixes. Until now a fix reached iOS students only with a new App Store version, weeks later, while the exercise could reject a correct answer.

From 0.7.2 the app can download a newer build of its exercise engine:
- JavaScript and CSS only, run in the app's WKWebView (PLA 3.3.1(B)); no native code.
- Published by us over HTTPS in the app's open-source repository: https://forge.apps.education.fr/coopmaths/mathalea-mobile/-/tree/main/releases/moteur
- Signed with our Ed25519 key; the app checks signature, size, SHA-256 and interface version, otherwise keeps the bundled engine.
- Used from the next launch, never during an activity.
An update only adds or fixes maths exercises: questions, answer fields, answer checking, corrections. The maths keyboard is part of each question: the engine shows the keys this question needs (digits, operations, letters of its expression); the app's own screens use the system keyboard. No change to the app's purpose, screens or features; no ads, purchases, tracking, data collection or permission. If you prefer, we can isolate the engine in a frame with no access to the app bridge.
To check: Réglages > À propos shows the engine version and "Rechercher une mise à jour du moteur".

PURPOSE
Free maths practice app for secondary school students (11 to 18), in French. It organises MathALÉA exercises into paths (levels and belts), corrects each answer, keeps progress on the device only and works offline. No account, login or paid content.

HOW TO TEST (no credentials)
a. Launch, choose "France", tap "Je n'ai pas de lien".
b. Explorer tab: open "Additionner et soustraire des relatifs" (5e), "Commencer", answer with the maths keyboard.
c. Teacher's class: on "Mes parcours", paste in "Lien de partage donné par ton enseignant", then "Ajouter le dossier":
https://forge.apps.education.fr/coopmaths/mathalea-mobile/-/raw/main/static/demo/parcours-demonstration.json
The QR code icon does the same with a QR code.
d. Path page > "Autres actions" > "Session partagée": code and QR code for another device.
e. Guideline 1.2: "Autres actions" > "Signaler un problème" (email to contact@coopmaths.fr with the path) and "Cacher ce parcours"; removing a folder in Réglages blocks that teacher.

NETWORK
No authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI service; no user data sent. Read-only downloads: Explorer paths (Infomaniak kDrive), teachers' shared folders, signed engine updates (forge.apps.education.fr). Date header of mathalea.fr and forge.apps.education.fr for paths opening on a date. Camera only for QR codes. Privacy: Data Not Collected.
