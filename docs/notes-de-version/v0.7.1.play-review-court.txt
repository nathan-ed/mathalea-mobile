No account. Exercises come from MathALÉA, our open-source exercise generator, updated daily. The app may download its newer engine (JS/CSS only, no native code) over HTTPS from our repo, Ed25519-signed and checked (signature, SHA-256, size), used at next launch. It only adds or fixes exercises; no sensitive native API is exposed. No ads, no data collection.
